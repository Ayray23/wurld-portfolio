VISITOR INFORMATION

Privacy & enquiries.

This portfolio is operated for Wurld (Adeniyi Waris), a painter based in Ile-Ife, Nigeria. This notice explains what happens when you contact the artist through the website.

Information you provide

Choosing Send to studio inbox sends your name, email address, enquiry category and message to the artist's website inbox. Wurld uses these details to read and reply to your request and discuss artworks, commissions or collaborations.

How information is handled

The information is stored in Firebase services used by this portfolio. Studio inbox access is restricted to approved artist accounts. Messages are retained while needed to handle your enquiry or relevant correspondence and may be deleted when no longer required.

WhatsApp and email

Choosing the WhatsApp or email buttons opens a draft in the respective app. You decide whether to send it, and that service's own privacy terms apply. The website does not automatically store those drafts in its studio inbox.

Security and spam prevention

Studio inbox submissions use anti-abuse measures including Firebase App Check and temporary rate-limit records. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can use WhatsApp or email instead of the studio inbox. To request access, correction or deletion of a stored enquiry, contact adeniyiw123@gmail.com.

Updates

This notice may change as the website develops. Updated 15 November 2026.
